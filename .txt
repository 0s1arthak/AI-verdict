Thank you both for presenting your compelling arguments regarding the activities of the individual known as Batman. 
The court has considered two critical perspectives:

The Defense presented Batman as a moral guardian fulfilling a societal need, particularly:
1. His strict adherence to a non-lethal code compared to traditional policing methods
2. Statistical evidence suggesting a correlation between his presence and reduced crime rates
3. The Bat-Signal itself implying at least partial governmental endorsement

The Prosecution countered with concerns about systemic issues:
1. Lack of official oversight leading to potential abuse of authority
2. Evidence of collateral damage and unintended harm to civilians
3. Claims that his actions incite copycat vigilantism rather than deterring crime
4. Arguments that Batman's rogue methods threaten civic order and accountability

Verdict: Not guilty of undermining the justice system, but guilty of operating outside legal channels. While Batman appears motivated by positive intentions, his methods remain extralegal. He is hereby ordered to:
1. Cease all vigilante activities in Gotham City
2. Partner formally with law enforcement through sanctioned channels
3. Receive proper training to minimize collateral damage should his services be officially requested

The complexity of Batman's actions reflects larger systemic issues within Gotham's justice system that require deeper examination. The court further recommends:
- Increased funding for law enforcement to better address systemic crime
- Establishment of a formal structure for citizen cooperation with police
- Investigation into methods to reduce both civic reliance on vigilante justice and the conditions that foster its emergence     

This decision acknowledges both Batman's commendable intentions and the dangers of unregulated vigilantism. Cooperation between all parties moving forward is encouraged.

This case is dismissed. The court is adjourned.
